Analysis

Overview

Mimic copies another player's combat behavior onto the local player. Two BooleanValue-with-Mode toggles define the two halves: Clicker (ClickerMimic) records the target's arm-swing cadence from the S0B animation stream and replays it as real clicks, and Rotations (RotationsMimic) mirrors the target's look direction with eased, jittered setAngles steps. Both halves activate only while the attack key is held. The wire footprint is the vanilla click path: each replayed click runs clickMouse, which swings (C0A) and attacks (C02), and the mirrored rotations land in the next C03 look fields.

ClickerMimic

A VERY_LOW PacketReceiveEvent watches S0B arm animations for entities other than the player, groups the tick deltas between swings per player name, and prints the recorded interval. On PreUpdateEvent with the attack key held, the mode pops the head of the recorded list, rejects intervals of zero ticks or 450ms and above (the while loop keeps re-rolling until an interval between 1 and 8 ticks is found), then waits half that interval and calls mc.clickMouse() with leftClickCounter set to 1. Replaying at half the recorded interval doubles the click rate; holding sneak clears the recording. The recorded stream is tick-quantized because it is read from ticksExisted, so even a human cadence is stored as whole-tick gaps.

RotationsMimic

With a target within 6 blocks and the attack key held, the mode mirrors the target's look: yaw becomes target.rotationYaw minus 180 and pitch becomes the negated target pitch, then both are eased through 150ms LINEAR Animation objects. A RotationUtil.move pass computes an overshoot toward the real target and adds it with a divisor of 1.05 when the yaw difference exceeds 90 degrees, otherwise 1.5, so the mirrored direction leans toward the target. The offset relative to the player's current look is jittered by plus or minus 5% of itself, and Render2D applies it through setAngles scaled by the mouse-sensitivity cube (the same 0.15 factor the mouse path uses), so the resulting rotation stream is eased, quantized and never a snap.

Wire presence

ClickerMimic's clickMouse produces the standard 1.8 attack order: swingItem first, then the C02 attack, so every replay emits one C0A and one C02. RotationsMimic never sends packets of its own; setAngles writes the view angles and the next C03PacketPlayer carries them as the server-visible look. The two halves compose into a player whose swing cadence and look motion statistically resemble the recorded victim's, which is the module's whole point: the stream looks like a different human's combat.

How it counteracts this module

FightSpeed caps the C02 rate at 16 CPS: it logs tick deltas of at most 4 ticks between consecutive non-INTERACT InteractEntityEvents, and every 10-sample window whose mean spacing implies more than 16 CPS punishes. The replay loop makes the rate the direct signal: intervals are replayed at half their recorded length, so a recorded 2-tick (100ms) cadence replays at 50ms, a flat 20 CPS, and a recorded 1-tick cadence replays at 25ms, 40 CPS.

The recorded cadence itself is tick-quantized, so the replay is a step function of 50ms multiples, exactly the low-jitter profile the check scores: deltas of one or two ticks read 20 or more CPS and every window punishes. The honest boundary: a recorded victim who clicks slowly produces intervals that replay under the cap, and the module's rate then sits where a human's would.

How it counteracts this module

FightSpeedConsistency punishes cadence regularity: 20-click window means above MIN_CPS 10 with a standard deviation under 0.05 across five windows. A periodic replay source produces window means that barely move, so the stdev test fires whenever the mean clears the gate.

The mimic is a mixed case. A victim with a regular cadence (another autoclicker, or a human clicking steadily) stores near-constant tick gaps, and the doubled replay keeps the window means constant, so the check fires at any mean above 10 CPS. A genuinely irregular human cadence stores varied gaps and replays them at scale, so the window means wander above 0.05 and the check stays quiet: the mimic reproduces the victim's own irregularity, which is the honest gap this entry documents.

How it counteracts this module
