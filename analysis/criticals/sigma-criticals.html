Analysis

Overview

Sigma 4.11's Criticals is a six-mode module (Packet, HPacket, Minis, HMinis, Hover, Jump) that injects C04 position packets from the pre-attack event and conditions every crit on the target's hurt resistance. The core doCrits() routine sends y + 0.0626 off-ground, an optional y + 0.0626 + 1e-11 second lift in HPacket mode, and a return-to-ground C04 at the original y that deliberately claims onGround false. Hover mode rewrites the motion event instead: it zeroes lastReportedPosY, lifts y by 1E-8 with onGround true, then decays the lift by 5E-15/4E-15 per tick. Minis and HMinis prepend an onGround-true C06 before a Killaura jump, and Jump mode just calls the real jump(). A HURTTIME setting (default 15) plus ground-tick, step and timer gates decide when doCrits runs.

Architecture

The module subscribes to five event types. EventAttack (pre-attack phase) is the trigger: Packet and HPacket call doCrits() only when the player's own ground checks pass (mc.thePlayer.onGround and MoveUtils.isOnGround(0.001)), the target's hurtResistantTime is at or below the HURTTIME setting (default 15), no step has happened in the last 20 ticks (lastStep.check(20)), either 200ms elapsed or the target is still hittable (hurtResistantTime > 0), the player is collided vertically, and more than one ground tick has accumulated. EventUpdate maintains groundTicks and implements Hover; EventJump prepends the onGround-true C06 for Minis/HMinis on the Killaura setup tick; EventStep resets the step timer and the Hover stage after each step-up; EventPacket zeroes the Hover stage on any S08 teleport and carries a half-written C03 interception that is effectively dead (the HPacket branch inside it is commented out). LongJump and Bhop being enabled blocks the attack path entirely.

Jump offsets and timing

doCrits() uses off = 0.0626 - a hair above the classic 0.0625 pixel, per the source comment '0.0625, 17.64e-8' - and sends C04(x, y + off, z, false), then, in HPacket mode, C04(x, y + off + 0.00000000001, z, false), then C04(x, y, z, false). All three claim off-ground, including the return move, so the server's fall distance does not reset at the bottom of the hop. Hover mode works on the motion event: it sets lastReportedPosY to 0 (removing the vanilla moved-too-quickly y reference), claims onGround true at y + 1E-8 on stage 0, then steps y down by 5E-15 and 4E-15 on stages 1 and 2 while claiming off-ground, recycling to stage 0 as soon as y reaches the real position. Minis/HMinis send a C06 at the current position with onGround true during the jump's pre-event so the client can jump even though its previous packets claimed air. Every S08 teleport and post-step event resets the Hover stage so the cycle restarts from the new ground.

Interaction with attacks

The C04s are sent from the pre-attack phase of EventAttack, so they are written to the send queue before Killaura's C02 - the server processes the 0.0626 lift, sees the off-ground return, and the attack that follows registers a crit with roughly 0.06 of fall distance. The hurt-time gating is the module's rhythm: it only crits when the victim's hurtResistantTime is below the HURRTIME threshold (default 15), i.e. when the victim is actually hittable again, which also spaces crits naturally. The groundTicks > 1 requirement forces at least two consecutive grounded ticks before a crit, and the step timer prevents critting out of a step-up. The Hypixel warning posted in onEnable and on every mode change ('Packet criticals doesn't bypass on Hypixel') is Sigma's own acknowledgment that the packet modes are tuned for less aggressive servers; Jump mode (a real jump) is the fallback for those.
