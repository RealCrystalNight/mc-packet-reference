Overview

Aimbot is a rotation-only assist driven from StrafeEvent: it picks the entity that passes the client's target filters, is visible through a vanilla ray trace, sits within range of its bounding box, and is closest in rotation difference, then steps the view toward an aim point at a limited turn speed. The module never touches packet classes; the smoothed rotation rides the player's own C03 look values and the C02 attacks the player's own clicks produce. Center mode aims at the box center, the default searchCenter mode aims at the visible box sample that requires the least rotation, and an optional jitter perturbs the view by up to one degree.

Target selection

onStrafe runs per movement tick. The filter chain requires EntityUtils.isSelected(entity, true), which applies the client's player and mob toggles plus AntiBot, NoHurt and friend checks; mc.thePlayer.canEntityBeSeen for a vanilla ray trace; getDistanceToEntityBox within the range setting (default 4.4); and a rotation difference within the FOV setting (default 180). The survivor set is reduced with minBy RotationUtils.getRotationDifference, so the target is the nearest in aim, not in space. OnClick mode gates the whole assist on a clickTimer that resets while the attack key is held and skips assist for 500ms after the last click, and Lock mode stops assisting once RotationUtils.isFaced reports the current view already covers the target.

Rotation smoothing

The aim point is either the box center via RotationUtils.getCenter plus toRotation with prediction, or the searchCenter result: searchCenter samples the bounding box at 0.1-step fractions (x and z from 0.15 to 0.85, y from 0.15 to 1.0), keeps samples within range that pass the visibility ray trace, and returns the sample whose rotation differs least from the player's current rotation. The target rotation is then fed to RotationUtils.limitAngleChange with a step limit of turnSpeed plus a fresh uniform 0-1 draw, so per-tick yaw and pitch deltas are capped at the setting plus up to one degree of noise, and rotation.toPlayer writes the result straight into rotationYaw and rotationPitch.

Gating and jitter

Jitter mode adds a random walk on top of the converged view: per tick, independent Random.nextBoolean draws decide whether yaw and pitch move and in which sign, each by up to one degree, with pitch clamped to the legal [-90, 90] range. Because the assist writes the local view, the next C03 carries the smoothed values, and any attack the player lands emits a C02 whose direction matches the aim point. The turnSpeed noise means the step size is never exactly constant, which is the module's hedge against fixed-step rotation detectors.

How it counteracts this module

Grim's Reach check computes the minimum reachable distance to the victim's hitbox via ReachUtils with the interaction attribute plus a movement allowance, passing borderline hits through an angle check. The aimbot's 4.4 default range enables C02 attacks at distances where a legit player could not land them, so attacks clicked past the survival reach bound flag even though the aim itself is precise; lowering range to 3.0 keeps the assist inside the envelope.
