Analysis

Overview

Mint B1's Disabler is a kitchen-sink packet interceptor: a single onEvent dispatcher branches on receive, send, motion and update events and applies whatever subset of its 45 settings are enabled. Modes are named for the anticheats and servers they target (Watchdog2020, Verus, Karhu, NCP, Spartan, Redesky, Warzone, Zonecraft, Hazel, PurplePrison, Matrix, Kauri, MineLatino, Supercraft) plus a row of generic primitives: C0F transaction corruption, C00 keepalive key rewriting, C0B entity-action spam, C07 digging spam, C08 placement spam, C13 abilities injection, C16 respawn spam, C18 spectate spoofs and C0C input spoofs. The dominant technique is queue-and-flush: packets are cancelled, parked in one of three queues, and released in bursts through sendPacketNoEvent on a timer, so the server receives everything late and out of phase. The file also carries a heavy load of dead code, empty stub modes and at least one guaranteed ClassCastException.

Architecture

All logic lives in one method: onEvent(Event) branches on instanceof across EventReceivePacket, EventSendPacket, EventMotion and EventUpdate. State is kept in three ConcurrentLinkedDeque packet queues (a general queue, a C0F-only queue, and a never-used C00-only queue), a shared Timer with two independent clocks, and a handful of flags. Cross-module communication is done through public statics: hypixelStrafe and desyncrot are written here and read elsewhere in Mint, and vulcanyaw is used as a scratch register between the NCP and Kauri modes.

Key techniques

The queue-and-flush pattern appears in nearly every mode: cancel the packet, park it, and release the backlog through sendPacketNoEvent on a timer (15s for Watchdog2020, 400ms for Verus, 1-2s random for Redesky/Spartan, 5412ms and 1337ms for Warzone's two queues, 690ms/2500ms for Zonecraft, 1550ms for VerusCombat, 505ms/200ms for MineLatino/Supercraft). Field corruption rewrites C0F uids to 1337 and ids to 69, C00 keys to 1337, and C13 packets to claim flying, creative, and invulnerable. Movement bypasses add constant y drift (Karhu 0.015625, Redesky 0.000125), send mirrored C04 position pairs 9 blocks ahead and behind (NCP), rewrite C04/C06 to void coordinates (PurplePrison), or alternate onGround flags (Zonecraft). The receive side cancels S08 teleports and re-sends them as C06 (Redesky, NCP) and cancels S12 velocity.

Code quality

The file is accretion-heavy: settings like HypixelTimerTest, Spartan, mineLatino and watchdog are declared with UI labels but their handlers are empty or absent; packetQueueC00Only, the x/y/z/yaw/pitch fields, transactiontype, placetype and NumberSetting are dead; and getXZDifference is called with packet.x twice (a copy-paste bug). The Hazel mode casts a C0F transaction to C00PacketKeepAlive, which throws ClassCastException the first time the branch runs, and the NCP module scan compares module names with == instead of equals(). The ModeDisplay setting is a 31-option mirror of every toggle, used only for the HUD label.
