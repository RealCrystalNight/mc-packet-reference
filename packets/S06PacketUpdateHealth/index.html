Notes

When health ≤ 0, the client shows the death screen overlay and sends C16PacketClientStatus(PERFORM_RESPAWN) on respawn button click.

Deep Dive

Role in the protocol

The player's authoritative health, food and saturation sync. EntityPlayerMP sends it on join and on every health, food or saturation change (line ~382), which in combat means roughly once per damage tick. The wire carries a float health (0-20, 0.0 means dead), a VarInt food level and a float saturation. The client treats it as truth: it drives the hearts, the hunger bar and the death screen, and there is no serverbound health packet, so the client cannot push health back.

Vanilla handling

handleUpdateHealth writes the three values into EntityPlayerSP (setPlayerSPHealth, foodStats) and, when health is at or below 0, arms the death-screen state. Respawn itself is a separate round trip: the button click sends C16PacketClientStatus with PERFORM_RESPAWN and the server answers with S07PacketRespawn. Because S06 is receive-only, every client-side health read is a mirror of the last packet.

Why clients touch it

The corpus shows two live integrations and one dead one. Sigma 4.11 Ranking treats S06 as a damage-confirmation clock: a health drop triggers a scan of loaded players for a swing animation within the last 70 ms and 9 blocks, which is how its hidden telemetry attributes deaths. Rise 6.1.30/6.2.4 StaffDetector carries an S06 branch that is fully commented out, and Sigma 4.11 HitIndicator imports the packet but leaves its packet branch empty. The classic Regen/AutoPot/AutoSoup family that keys off S06 thresholds in other client generations does not appear here; in these 8 clients those modules poll local health on UpdateEvent instead.

Exploit surface

The classic abuse is receive-side cancellation: a module that swallows S06 never learns it died, so the death screen never shows while the server keeps sending damage updates and eventually kicks the dead player. No module in the corpus does this. Anti-cheats flag nothing on S06 because the client cannot influence it; the only measurable cost is bandwidth during high-tick combat.

Server-Side Handling

EntityPlayerMP pushes S06PacketUpdateHealth(health, foodLevel, saturation) whenever the player's stats change (line ~382 in the MCP builds), which covers join, damage, regeneration, hunger and saturation drift. The server never validates the packet stream because it is the sole author; the values are copied verbatim from the player's own stats. The death path is two-sided: the client arms the death screen from S06 health <= 0, and the respawn is driven by the client's C16 PERFORM_RESPAWN.

Protocol Analysis

Fields in order: float health, VarInt food, float saturation. There is no serverbound health packet, so any module that wants to fake health must do it by suppressing the clientbound packet (cosmetic only) or by manipulating the server's damage logic through other packets. S06 arrives on the main thread and its writes are unconditional, so a cancelling module must intercept before the vanilla handler.

Anti-Cheat Landscape

Anti-cheats treat S06 as render-state sync and measure nothing on it; the client cannot spoof health serverbound, so there is no flag surface. The practical detection risk belongs to modules that act on S06 (Sigma Ranking's HTTP telemetry, Rise's commented staff heuristic), which are privacy/ToS concerns rather than movement or combat flags.

implementation Implementation Cases

The client's only authoritative health source, sent on join and on every damage, food or saturation change. The corpus uses it as a damage-confirmation clock (Sigma Ranking telemetry) and as abandoned scaffolding (Rise StaffDetector, Sigma HitIndicator); the Regen/AutoPot threshold pattern is absent from these 8 clients, which poll local health on ticks instead.

Ranking (Sigma 4.11)

Hidden combat telemetry that uses S06 health drops to attribute deaths and prosecutions to specific players.

On EventPacket, when S06 reports health at or below the client's current health, Ranking scans the loaded entity list for players that are not the local player, have a swing animation (S0BPacketAnimation) timestamp within the last 70 ms and sit within 9 blocks; each match becomes a prosecutor entity. health <= 0 calls onDeath, which resolves outstanding kill/death records and ships them through sendStat. The S06 packet is the timing anchor: the 70 ms window is measured from animation timestamp to damage confirmation.
