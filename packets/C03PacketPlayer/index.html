Deep Dive

Role in the protocol

C03PacketPlayer is the abstract base of the serverbound movement family: four registered packet ids (0x03 bare, 0x04 position, 0x05 look, 0x06 pos+look) share one class with subclasses, and the vanilla client emits one of them every tick from EntityPlayerSP.onUpdateWalkingPlayer. The payload is a single onGround byte plus optional x/y/z doubles and yaw/pitch floats. Every server-side movement decision derives from it: position integration, ground state, fall distance, rotation, and the server's own movement validation. It is the most-sent packet in the play state and by far the most-abused one in the 8-client corpus, which references it in over 300 mined locations.

Vanilla handling

NetHandlerPlayServer.processPlayer snapshots lastPos, computes the squared delta, and arms a hasMoved flag on the first small movement. Position values beyond +-3.0E7 on X or Z kick with "Illegal position". A reported Y of -999.0 disables the movement flag entirely (the client sends it when falling out of the world). The vanilla anti-cheat is the "moved too quickly" check: the sum of min(|delta|, |motion|) squared per axis must stay under 100.0, otherwise the player is teleported back with a warning. A residual delta over 0.0625 squared after moveEntity logs "moved wrongly" and snaps the player back. If the client reports onGround=false while the server thinks the player is grounded and Y is rising, the server calls jump(). Fall damage is applied from the server's own simulated fall state, which is what ground spoofing attacks.

Why clients touch it

The corpus splits into five families. Ground-state spoofers: NoFall in all eight clients, plus FastUse and Regen that send C03(true) or bare C03() to force the server-side regen tick. Movement engines: Flight (Nekoware, Rise 5.99, the 13+ mode Rise 6.x flight suite, Sigma, Spicy, Tenacity), Speed and Bhop (Rise 5.99, Rise 6.x, Sigma, Spicy), Step (Memeware, Nekoware, Rise 5.99, Rise 6.x, Sigma), Phase (Memeware, Rise 5.99, Rise 6.x NormalPhase, Sigma). Buffer and replay: Blink in Rise 5.99, Rise 6.x components, Sigma, Spicy and Tenacity. Position injection: Criticals offset ladders, TPAura and Infinite path walks, Damage utilities, AntiVoid, LongJump, and teleport modules. Disablers: Memeware, Rise 5.99, the Rise 6.x per-server suite, Sigma, Spicy and Tenacity all rewrite, cancel or re-inject C03 to break anti-cheat position timing.

Exploit surface

onGround is a single trusted bit that drives server-side jump physics and fall-damage accumulation: NoFall reports ground every tick so the server's entity never accrues fall distance. The 100.0 and 0.0625 squared-delta gates are the real vanilla anti-cheat, so every movement module must keep reported deltas under them or accept the teleport-back. The -999.0 Y sentinel is a free pass for vertical state: modules and commands use it to reset position without moving the player. Disablers weaponize the packet stream itself: cancelling C03 while Hypixel's watchdog is armed freezes the player server-side, and rewriting C06 into C04 (or the reverse) breaks anti-cheats that count packet-type ratios. Rotation is never validated against motion in vanilla, so spinbot and rotation-spoof modules can report any yaw/pitch.

Server-Side Handling

processPlayer runs on the server thread and, for position-carrying variants, compares the packet position against the stored lastPos. The hasMoved gate requires the first reported movement to be under 0.25 squared before any validation starts. The "moved too quickly" check uses min(|delta|, |motion|) per axis, so large instantaneous deltas are masked by small motion values; the check teleports back via setPlayerLocation instead of kicking. "moved wrongly" fires when the post-integration residual exceeds 0.0625 squared and the player is not sleeping or in creative, also teleporting back. When the client reports onGround=false while the server-side player is grounded and Y is rising, the server calls jump(), which is the mechanism both criticals and the watchdog-disabler +0.16/+0.07 packet pairs exploit. The flight check runs when the player has no block collision under the bounding box and flight is disabled, kicking on sustained airborne motion.

Protocol Analysis

Four distinct ids map to one class: C03PacketPlayer (onGround only), C04PacketPlayerPosition (+x,y,z), C05PacketPlayerLook (+yaw,pitch), C06PacketPlayerPosLook (+both). The moving flag (field_149480_h) is set by the position constructors and the rotating flag by the look constructors; writePacketData emits the onGround byte first, then the doubles and floats conditionally. The -999.0 Y convention is a wire-level sentinel: the client sends C06 with y=-999 when falling out of the world, and the server clears the moving flag when it sees it. EntityPlayerSP selects the subclass by what changed this tick, and NetHandlerPlayClient answers S08 teleports with a C06 confirming the new position.

Anti-Cheat Landscape

NCP checks ground-state consistency (onGround true while falling), horizontal acceleration for speed, vertical acceleration for flight, and packet gaps for blink. Verus and AAC correlate rotation with motion, which is why Rise 6.x's PacketNoRotate rewrites C06 into C04 to strip rotations. Hypixel's watchdog is fought with the ground-truth +0.16/+0.07 jump-flag pair followed by full C03 cancellation for the watchdog window, disarmed by S08. Grim and Vulcan target packet-order and buffer abuse, countered by Rise 6.x modes like BufferAbuseFlight and the ConvertMovingPackets disabler. Timer-based flight and bhop (Spicy's 26.5 tps) flag timer checks unless the keepalive and transaction streams are delayed in lockstep, which is the disabler's second job.
